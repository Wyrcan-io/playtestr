## Qualification conclusion and invalidation

There is no unexplained false pass/failure, destructive cleanup, unbounded
execution, managed-process survivor, or unsafe artifact result in accepted
evidence. Target-rendered secrets may still appear in admitted evidence;
automatic redaction is not claimed.

Executable hashes are the evidence identities. A source, dependency, compiler,
linker/build flag, or embedded-version change refreezes and invalidates affected
results. Release-story and documentation-only commits after the freeze retained
evidence because the candidate bytes did not change. Archive/layout/install
changes would require their affected gates again. Raw ignored evidence is local
and Actions artifacts expire after 14 days; this sanitized record, manifests,
hashes, failure classification, and reproduction inputs are durable.
