# Measured authoring experience

These are prepared agent routes with synthetic fixtures and an available target.
They measure command/control counts, not unfamiliar-user success or active human
time. Failed initial attempts remain in their original windows. The proposed
engineering target was at most five invocations and twenty recorder controls.

The public recorder now captures reviewed screen and file/directory expectations,
replays in fresh managed state, exports an ordinary spec, and rejects unresolved
failed capture/completion checks. Selected expectation updates and suffix
rerecording preserve other reviewed expectations. The per-project development
admissions retain actual maintenance, diagnosis and relocation evidence; these
earlier source/rc.4 authoring measurements are not relabeled as rc.5 repetitions.

The exact rc.5 [archive experiences](archive-experience/README.md) separately
exercise installation, record/test/report, corruption and recovery, upgrade and
rollback. The [delivered CI triplet](archive-experience/delivered-ci-verification.json)
demonstrates a generated free hosted route, a real saved-file regression with
passing prior screen/exit evidence, retained diagnosis, and recovery. It used a
source-built runner at the frozen source, not the private archive executable, and
did not exercise a real pull-request event.

No route required handwritten Playtestr JSON for the measured first recording.
Target preparation, dependency acquisition, prior source knowledge, repeated
exposure and warm prerequisites limit comparisons. A shorter fresh route does
not erase its longer initial window. No measured unfamiliar-user time, success
rate, adoption, or commercial conclusion is available.
